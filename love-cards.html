My Dearest Tabrez,

Pata hai, jab main aapse pehli baar mili thi na, jaana... ✨ mujhe bilkul nahi pata tha ki ek normal si mulaqat meri zindagi ki sabse khaas yaad ban jayegi. Aaj bhi us din ko yaad karke mera dil muskura deta hai.

Pehli baar jab maine aapko dekha tha, to mera dil aise dhadakne laga jaise pehli baar dhadak raha ho. Aas-paas itne log aur itni awaazein thi, par meri nazar bas aap par hi ruk gayi thi, jaan. 👀💗

Us waqt thoda darr bhi lagta tha... kahin aapse milne ke baad aap dobara door na ho jao. Lekin aapki smile, aapki baatein, aapki aankhein aur aapka woh simple sa hona... sab kuch mujhe bahut pyaara laga tha, betuuu. 🥺

Sach kahun to us din ke baad kuch bhi pehle jaisa nahi raha. Har waqt bas aapka khayal aata tha. Kab dobara milenge, kab baat hogi... raat ko sone se pehle bhi aap yaad aate, aur subah uthte hi mera pehla khayal bhi aap hi hote, babyy. 🫶

Aapse milne ke baad mujhe samajh aaya ki kismat bhi kitni khoobsurat hoti hai. Itne saare logon mein, itni badi duniya mein humara milna... shayad Allah ke paas pehle se likha hua tha, meri jaan. 🌙

Kaash woh pehli mulaqat thodi aur lambi hoti... kaash main aapko thoda aur dekh leti, thodi aur baatein kar leti. 🥀 Lekin shayad achha hi hua, warna usi din apna dil aapko de deti. 🤭

Aap meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat mulaqat ho, husband jii. 💞 Aapse milna meri zindagi ka best moment tha, bacchaa... aur aapse milte rehna meri sabse badi khushi hai. Ab bas hamesha saath rehna hai, babu. 😽❤️

I Love You, Husband Jii
